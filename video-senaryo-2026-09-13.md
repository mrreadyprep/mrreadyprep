## Sahne Kırılımı

**Sahne 1 (0-2 sn) — Hook:**
Görsel: telefon ekranında bir cümle sesi çalıyor, kişi tekrar dinlemeye çalışırcasına ekrana dokunuyor ama hiçbir şey olmuyor.
On-screen metin (kırmızı, ❌ ile): **"Listen and Repeat: you only hear it ONCE ❌"**

**Sahne 2 (2-5 sn) — Bağlam:**
On-screen metin: **"7 sentences. Played one time each. No text on screen. No prep time. You repeat right after the beep."**

**Sahne 3 (5-14 sn) — 3 adımlı strateji (numaralı liste, yeşil):**
1️⃣ **Listen for the stress and rhythm, not just the words** — that's what's actually being scored.
2️⃣ **Don't pause to "remember" it perfectly** — start speaking the instant the beep hits; you get 8-12 seconds, and that window doesn't wait for you.
3️⃣ **Grammar and content don't matter here** — it's not graded on ideas, just how closely your pronunciation and intonation match what you heard.
