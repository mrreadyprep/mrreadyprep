## 2. "5 Habits That Actually Raise Your Speaking Score"

**Slide 1 (Cover):** Stuck at the same Speaking score? It's probably not what you think.

**Slide 2:** 1️⃣ Time yourself for real. 45 seconds, every time. Practicing without the pressure of the clock gives you a false sense of readiness.

**Slide 3:** 2️⃣ Record your answers. Listen back 1-2 days later — that's how you catch the hesitations you didn't notice in the moment.

**Slide 4:** 3️⃣ Start counting your fillers. "Um," "like," "you know" — when the count drops, you're actually improving.

**Slide 5:** 4️⃣ Memorize a structure, not an answer. Position + reason + example. Let the content change, keep the structure fixed.

**Slide 6:** 5️⃣ Don't fear silence. A 1-2 second pause reads far better than filling the gap with filler words.

**Slide 7 (CTA):** mrreadyprep lets you record and play back your own answers — habit #2, automated. Link in bio.

## 3. "The #1 Mistake in Academic Discussion"

**Slide 1 (Cover):** Everyone makes the same mistake on TOEFL's newest Writing task.

**Slide 2:** Academic Discussion replaced Independent Writing: you get 10 minutes to respond to two students' opinions on a discussion board, in 100+ words.

**Slide 3:** ❌ The most common mistake: summarizing what the two students already said.

**Slide 4:** ✅ Instead: state your own clear opinion, back it with a specific reason + example.

**Slide 5:** Simple template: Opinion → Reason → Example → (agree/disagree with the other student + why) → Short wrap-up.
