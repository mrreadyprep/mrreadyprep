The 2026 TOEFL iBT reports your results on a **1–6 band scale, in half-point steps**, for each section and overall. The bands are aligned with CEFR levels (A1–C2), so it's easier to see what your score means in practice.

**How to find your target score:**
1. Make a list of the universities or programs you're applying to.
2. Check each one's admissions page for its TOEFL requirement. Many now list band scores, and some still list the old 0–120 number.
3. Your target is the **highest** requirement on your list, including any section minimums.

Once you know your target, you know how far you need to go, and that's what should shape your study plan.

## Mail 3: 4. gün

**Konu:** The Reading task most people underestimate
**Önizleme:** "Complete the Words" looks easy. Here's how to get it right.

Hi {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "there" }},

The new Reading section opens with **Complete the Words**: a short paragraph where some words are missing their second half, and you fill in the missing letters.

It looks simple, but the clock is tight. Three tips:

1. **Read the whole sentence first.** Grammar tells you a lot: a missing word after "the" is probably a noun, and after "has" it's probably a past participle.
2. **Use the letters you're given.** The first half of the word narrows your options a lot, so trust it.
3. **Check your spelling.** A word that's almost right is still marked wrong.

You can practise this exact task type, with a timer, on MRReadyPrep.
