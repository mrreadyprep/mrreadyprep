### SCENARIO 2: Email Open Rate Below Target

**Trigger:** Email open rate <35% on first send, or trending down Week 2

**Root Causes to Investigate:**
- Subject lines not compelling
- Email sent at suboptimal time
- Email content not matching signup promise
- Sender reputation issues (spam folder)

**Immediate Actions (24 hours):**
- [ ] A/B test 2 new subject lines on next email (50/50 split)
  - Variant A: Personalized with name ("Mehmet's Free TOEFL Guide")
  - Variant B: Curiosity-driven ("This TOEFL Format Change Will Surprise You")
- [ ] Review email content: Remove any spammy language, shorten copy
- [ ] Check sender reputation: Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC records
- [ ] Review spam folder: See if emails are being filtered
- [ ] Adjust send time: Try sending at different hour (e.g., 9 AM vs. 6 PM)

**Short-Term Fixes (Week 2):**
- [ ] Segment email list by source & send time
- [ ] Test different email templates (minimal design vs. rich design)
- [ ] Add preview text (first 50 characters visible before open)
- [ ] Include compelling subject line hooks:
  - Social proof: "1,000+ students are using this..."
  - Question: "Is your TOEFL study method working?"
  - Urgency: "2026 format changes start soon..."
- [ ] Reduce email frequency if unsubscribe rate is rising

**Measurement:**
- Compare open rates after subject line A/B test
- Target: 40%+ open rate by end of Week 2
- If still <30%: Review email list quality & segment strategy

## NOTES & BEST PRACTICES

**Messaging Consistency:**
- Use same value prop across blog, email, social, landing page
- Reinforce 2026 format positioning in every touchpoint
- Include CTA in every piece of content

**Content Quality:**
- All blog articles should be 800+ words, well-researched
- Include at least 1 visual per article (infographic, chart, or screenshot)
- Include 1 internal link per article (to another article or signup page)

**Email Best Practices:**
- Personalize with first name where possible
- Use conversational tone, not salesy
- Keep subject lines under 50 characters
- Mobile-optimize all email templates
- Include unsubscribe link (legal requirement)

**Social Media Best Practices:**
- Post at optimal times per platform (research your audience)
- Use platform-native features (Reels for Instagram, TikTok for TikTok)
- Respond to all comments within 24 hours
- Use relevant hashtags (8-15 per post)
- Engage with other creators' content daily

**GA4 Tracking:**
- Verify UTM parameters are firing on all links
- Set up conversion events for blog signup, trial signup, etc.
- Create custom audiences based on user behavior
- Review funnels: Blog → Email → Trial → Paid

**Team Responsibilities (Suggested):**
- Content Marketing Manager: Blog articles, email copywriting
- Social Media Manager: Social posts, community engagement
- Analyst: Metrics tracking, A/B testing, reporting
- Product Manager: Trial UX, onboarding, conversion optimization
