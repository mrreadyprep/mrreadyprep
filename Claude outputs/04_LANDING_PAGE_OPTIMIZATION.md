## BOUNCE POINT ANALYSIS

### Key Performance Indicators to Track

**1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)**
- Time on Hero: Track how long visitors stay before scrolling
- Click-through rate on primary CTA
- Scroll depth: % of visitors scrolling past hero

**Current Risk:** Generic hero messaging may not establish value immediately

**2. Value Proposition Section**
- Do visitors understand what MRReadyPrep does in 5 seconds?
- Which benefits resonate most? (Track with scroll events)

**Current Risk:** Too much text; key differentiator not clear

**3. Social Proof Section**
- Engagement with testimonials/reviews
- Screenshot clicks (student success stories)

**Current Risk:** May lack credibility indicators if testimonials aren't prominent

**4. CTA Placement & Copy**
- Primary CTA above fold (free trial)
- Secondary CTA mid-page (email signup)
- Trust signals nearby (money-back guarantee, no credit card needed)

**Current Risk:** CTAs may be weak or unclear

**5. Mobile Responsiveness**
- Mobile bounce rate vs. desktop
- Mobile conversion rate vs. desktop

**Current Risk:** Forms may be difficult on mobile; could drive bounces

**6. Form Friction**
- How many fields does the trial signup require?
- Do multi-step forms perform better than single-step?

**Current Risk:** Excessive form fields → abandonment

## A/B TESTING ROADMAP

### Test 1: Hero Section CTA Copy (Week 1)

**Current Version (A):**
```
Button Text: "Start Your Free Trial"
```

**Test Version (B):**
```
Button Text: "Get 14 Days Free (No Credit Card)"
```

**Hypothesis:** Adding reassurance ("No Credit Card") lowers friction and increases clicks

**Measurement:** 
- Button click rate (A vs. B)
- Trial signup completion rate
- Statistical significance: 500+ clicks minimum before concluding

**Expected Improvement:** 8-15% CTR increase if hypothesis correct

## FORM OPTIMIZATION

### Simplified Trial Signup Form

**Field 1: Email**
- Type: Email input
- Placeholder: "your@email.com"
- Required: Yes
- Validation: Real-time email validation

**Field 2: Target Score**
- Type: Radio buttons or dropdown
- Options:
  - [ ] Just pass (85+)
  - [ ] Competitive (100+)
  - [ ] Highly competitive (110+)
  - [ ] Unsure
- Required: No (but incentivize with "helps us personalize your plan")

**Button:** "Create My Free Account"

**Post-Signup (Do NOT ask before):**
Once they confirm email, then ask:
- Current score
- Time available
- Primary goal
- How they found you

This "progressive profiling" reduces friction while still collecting data.

## COMMON OPTIMIZATION MISTAKES (What NOT to Do)

**❌ Mistake 1: Too Many Tests at Once**
- Running 5 tests simultaneously makes it impossible to know which change drove results
- Recommendation: One major test per week

**❌ Mistake 2: Stopping Test Too Early**
- Need minimum statistical significance (usually 500+ conversions minimum)
- Recommendation: Run each test minimum 2 weeks, 500+ clicks

**❌ Mistake 3: Optimizing the Wrong Metric**
- Focusing on pageviews instead of conversions
- Recommendation: Always optimize for revenue/conversions, not vanity metrics

**❌ Mistake 4: Not Tracking User Journey**
- Knowing someone converted without knowing the path they took
- Recommendation: Track events for each major section/interaction

**❌ Mistake 5: Ignoring Mobile Users**
- Desktop optimization that doesn't translate to mobile
- Recommendation: Always test on actual devices, not just browser emulator
