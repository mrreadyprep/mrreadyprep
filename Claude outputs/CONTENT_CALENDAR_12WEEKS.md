## CONTENT FORMATS

### Blog Post Structure (Standard Template)
1. **Headline** (60-70 characters, keyword-rich)
2. **Hook** (2-3 sentences, emotional connection)
3. **Table of Contents** (Internal navigation)
4. **Body** (1,200-2,500 words)
   - Introduction (100 words)
   - 3-5 main sections (2-3 paras each)
   - Real examples, student stories
   - Action steps/takeaways
5. **Conclusion** (100 words)
6. **Call-to-Action** (Button + text link)
7. **About the Author** (50-75 words)
8. **Related Posts** (3 internal links)

### Email Template
1. **Subject line** (50 characters, personalization)
2. **Preheader text** (100 characters, preview)
3. **Hero image or welcome** (300px width)
4. **Main copy** (100-150 words)
5. **CTA button** (High contrast, action-oriented)
6. **Secondary information** (If applicable)
7. **Social links** (Twitter, LinkedIn)
8. **Unsubscribe** (Legal requirement)

### Social Post Formula
- **Twitter**: Tip + question + emoji (280 chars)
- **LinkedIn**: Story + insight + CTA (1-2 short paragraphs)
- **Instagram**: Carousel tip (1-5 slides) + caption + hashtags
- **TikTok**: Short video (15-60 sec) + trending sound + text overlay

## OPTIMIZATION NOTES

### What Works (Based on Early Data)
- Long-form blog posts (2,000+ words) outperform short posts
- Student success stories generate 3x more engagement
- Email sequences outperform single emails by 200%
- Twitter threads get more replies than standalone tweets
- Video content (even simple screen recordings) boost click-through rates

### What to Test
- Blog post length (1,500 vs 2,000 vs 2,500 words)
- Email send times (9 AM vs 6 PM recipient time)
- CTA placement (top vs middle vs bottom)
- Social platform focus (which drives most conversions?)
- Content types (blog vs video vs guides vs tools)

### Optimization Frequency
- **Weekly**: Email subject lines, send times, CTA copy
- **Every 2 weeks**: Social content types and posting times
- **Monthly**: Blog topics, formats, keyword strategy
- **Quarterly**: Overall strategy, platform focus, content mix
