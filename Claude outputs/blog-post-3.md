# TOEFL 2026 "Read in Daily Life": Sample Questions & Expert Tips

*Published: October 2026 | Reading time: 7 minutes*

## What is "Read in Daily Life"?

One of the biggest changes in TOEFL 2026 is the new "Read in Daily Life" task in the Reading section.

**It's not academic.** It's authentic English from real-world contexts:
- Restaurant menus
- Emails from landlords
- Parking signs
- Product reviews
- Announcements
- Return policies

The purpose: Test whether you can understand **everyday English**, not just scholarly texts.

---

## Why This Matters

**Old TOEFL:** "Read a passage about Renaissance art history..."
**New TOEFL:** "Read a restaurant menu and answer questions about it."

This shifts the test to real-world comprehension. Good news? These passages are **usually easier** than academic texts if you read naturally (not overthink).

---

## The Format

**Passage:** 50-150 words (short)
**Questions:** 3-4 multiple-choice questions
**Time:** ~3-4 minutes per passage
**Difficulty:** Easier than academic reading (vocab is simpler)

## Sample "Read in Daily Life" Passages

### Sample 1: Restaurant Menu

```
BELLA'S ITALIAN BISTRO
Lunch Menu (11am - 3pm)

APPETIZERS
Bruschetta - $8 (tomato, basil, olive oil)
Calamari Fritti - $12 (fried squid with marinara)

PASTA DISHES
Spaghetti Carbonara - $14
Penne al Arrabbiata - $13
(Gluten-free pasta available +$2)

DESSERTS
Tiramisu - $7
Gelato (3 flavors) - $5

NOTE: We use peanut oil. Inform server of allergies.
Gratuity not included in bill.
```

**Question 1:** According to the menu, which dish costs $12?
A) Bruschetta
B) Calamari Fritti
C) Penne al Arrabbiata
D) Tiramisu

**Answer:** B (Calamari Fritti - $12)

**Question 2:** If you want gluten-free pasta, how much extra will it cost?
A) $2
B) $5
C) $7
D) $13

**Answer:** A (+$2 as stated)

**Question 3:** Based on the note, what should customers do if they have allergies?
A) Avoid all dishes with oil
B) Order only appetizers
C) Inform the server
D) Call ahead the day before

**Answer:** C (Inform server of allergies)

### Sample 2: Email from Landlord

```
Subject: Apartment Lease Renewal

Dear Tenant,

Your current lease expires on December 31, 2026. If you wish to renew for another year, 
please sign the attached lease agreement and return it by November 15, 2026. 

The renewal rate will increase by 5% due to market conditions. Your new rent will be $1,050/month.

If you do NOT wish to renew, please provide 60 days written notice before your lease expires 
(by November 1, 2026).

Late fees: $50/day for any rent paid after the 5th of the month.

Please contact the office if you have questions.

Best regards,
Building Management
```

**Question 1:** When does the current lease expire?
A) November 1, 2026
B) November 15, 2026
C) December 31, 2026
D) January 1, 2027

**Answer:** C (December 31, 2026)

**Question 2:** What will the new rent be if the tenant renews?
A) $1,000/month
B) $1,050/month
C) $1,100/month
D) Not specified

**Answer:** B ($1,050/month)

**Question 3:** What is the deadline to notify the landlord if you do NOT wish to renew?
A) November 1, 2026
B) November 15, 2026
C) December 1, 2026
D) December 31, 2026

**Answer:** A (60 days before lease expires; expires Dec 31, so deadline is Nov 1)

**Question 4:** What is the late fee if rent is not paid by the 5th of the month?
A) $50 per day
B) $50 one time
C) 5% of monthly rent
D) Included in renewal terms

**Answer:** A ($50/day)

### Sample 3: Product Return Policy (from a website)

```
AMAZON-STYLE RETURN POLICY

Returns are easy. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, return it within 30 days of delivery 
for a full refund.

ITEMS THAT CAN BE RETURNED:
- Clothing, shoes, accessories (tags must be attached)
- Electronics (opened or unopened)
- Books, DVDs, games

ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED:
- Perishable food
- Underwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Custom-made items
- Items more than 30 days old

HOW TO RETURN:
1. Go to "Your Orders" on our website
2. Select the item
3. Choose "Return"
4. Print the return shipping label
5. Pack the item and drop it off at a carrier location

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receipt.
```

**Question 1:** Which of these items CAN be returned?
A) Perishable food
B) Underwear
C) Custom-made items
D) Clothing with tags attached

**Answer:** D (Clothing with tags attached - listed under "ITEMS THAT CAN BE RETURNED")

**Question 2:** Why can't underwear be returned?
A) It's too old
B) For hygiene reasons
C) It's custom-made
D) No reason is given

**Answer:** B (For hygiene reasons)

**Question 3:** How long does it take to receive a refund after the item is received?
A) 30 days
B) 5-7 business days
C) 5-7 days (calendar days)
D) 30 business days

**Answer:** B (5-7 business days)

## Expert Tips for "Read in Daily Life"

### Tip 1: Read the Question First (Sometimes)

For short passages, read the passage first. It's fast.

For longer passages with 4+ questions, skim the questions first so you know what to look for.

### Tip 2: Look for Exact Matches

These passages test **comprehension, not inference**.

Example:
- Passage: "Return within 30 days"
- Question: "How many days do you have to return?"
- Answer: Look for the number 30. Don't infer. Match.

### Tip 3: Pay Attention to Details

- Dates (November 1 vs. November 15)
- Numbers ($50 vs. $1,050)
- Conditions ("tags must be attached")
- Negatives ("CANNOT be returned")

### Tip 4: Watch for Traps

**Example trap question:**
Passage: "Gelato (3 flavors) - $5"
Question: "How many flavors of gelato are available?"
A) 5
B) 3 ← Correct (3 flavors)
C) Unlimited
D) Not specified

(Many test takers pick A, confusing the price $5 with the number of flavors.)

### Tip 5: Use Context for Unknown Words

If you see a word you don't know, use context.

Example:
"Inform server of allergies" → "allergies" = something bad with food (even if you don't know the English word)

## Practice Strategy

### Week 1-2: Get Familiar

- Do 5-10 "Read in Daily Life" passages per day
- Don't time yourself. Read naturally.
- Check answers. Understand why you missed any.

### Week 3-4: Build Speed

- Time yourself: 3-4 min per passage
- Still aim for 95%+ accuracy
- Focus on detail (numbers, dates, conditions)

### Week 5+: Full Sections

- Do "Read in Daily Life" as part of full Reading sections
- Maintain accuracy + speed

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Overthinking:** "Read in Daily Life" is straightforward. Don't add complexity.
❌ **Guessing on details:** Numbers, dates, names are exact. Match them.
❌ **Ignoring conditions:** "Tags must be attached" is important. "Tags optional" changes the answer.
❌ **Rushing:** Short passage ≠ fast reading. Accuracy first.
