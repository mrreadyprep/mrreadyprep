Preparation tips

Skim before you read the questions
Get a sense of the passage's structure and main idea first, then go back for detail and inference questions — it's faster than reading question-by-question.
Watch for paraphrased answers
Correct answers rarely repeat the passage word-for-word. Practice recognizing the same idea expressed with different vocabulary.
Build academic word families
Complete the Words and vocabulary-in-context questions reward knowing related word forms (e.g. analyze / analysis / analytical), not just single definitions.
Budget your time per question
Reading passages are timed as a set, not per question — decide roughly how long to spend on each question type so one hard question doesn't eat your whole passage.
