Preparation tips

Use a simple structure every time
State your answer in one sentence, then give one or two clear reasons with a brief example — a predictable structure keeps you fluent under time pressure.
Prioritize clarity over complex vocabulary
Natural pacing and clear pronunciation score better than forcing in advanced words you're not confident using correctly.
Practice out loud, not silently
Speaking tasks are scored on delivery, so rehearsing in your head doesn't train the skill that's actually being measured.
Record yourself and listen back
It's the fastest way to notice filler words, pacing issues, or unclear pronunciation you wouldn't otherwise catch.
