ETS TOEFL TestReady — the official option

TestReady is ETS's own prep portal, and that's a real advantage: the practice questions, guides, and progress tracking come directly from the organization that writes and scores the actual exam. There's no question about whether the material matches what you'll see on test day, because it's built by the same team. The tradeoff is that ETS doesn't publish TestReady's pricing as a simple flat monthly number the way most third-party tools do, and it's less oriented around instant, granular AI feedback on every practice attempt — it's closer to an official study companion than a full adaptive practice platform.

Best fit: someone who wants zero doubt that their materials are 100% authentic and doesn't mind piecing together pricing/bundles directly from ETS.

Magoosh TOEFL — the established paid course

Magoosh has been in test prep for a long time and it shows in the polish: 100+ video lessons, over 2,000 practice questions, and up to 10 full-length official-style practice tests on its premium plan, plus a stated +4 point score guarantee and a 7-day refund window. It's a genuinely comprehensive course, especially if you want structured video lessons walking you through strategy rather than jumping straight into practice. The cost is the main tradeoff: $109 for a single month is a real commitment, and there's no ongoing free tier — just a 7-day trial before you're deciding whether to pay.

Best fit: someone who learns best from video instruction and has the budget for a premium course, and who wants a single intensive study block rather than months of casual practice.

mrreadyprep — built specifically around the 2026 format

mrreadyprep's practice exercises are built around the exact task types ETS introduced in the January 2026 redesign — Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life, Build a Sentence, Listen and Repeat, Take an Interview, and the rest — scored instantly on the same 1–6, 0.5-point CEFR-aligned scale the real exam now uses. Every account gets free access to a limited set of items across all four skills with no card required, and Premium ($25/month, or as low as $16.67/month on the 6-month plan, with the first month automatically 50% off) unlocks the full question bank, all 20 full-length mock tests, and AI scoring across Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking. AI Tutor Chat is free on every account, Premium or not.

Best fit: someone who wants frequent, low-friction practice with instant feedback at a price that supports daily practice over weeks or months, rather than one expensive intensive push.

How to actually decide

If budget isn't a major constraint and you want material with zero ambiguity about authenticity, TestReady is worth adding alongside whatever else you use, precisely because it's the one option here written by ETS itself. If you learn best from structured video walkthroughs and can commit to an intensive month, Magoosh's course format is hard to beat. If you want to practice consistently over time — a bit of Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking most days, with instant scores telling you where you actually stand — that's the specific gap mrreadyprep is built for, and it's the only one of the three with a genuinely free ongoing tier to start with before you pay anything.
