Preparation tips

Sit the whole test in one sitting
Mock tests are most useful when taken under real conditions — no pausing to look things up, and no splitting sections across days.
Review mistakes the same day
Errors and reasoning are still fresh right after a test, which makes the review far more useful than coming back a week later.
Track score trends, not one result
A single mock test score can vary — look at the trend across a few tests to judge real progress.
Save your strongest mock test day for last
Use your final mock test closest to your real exam date, once you've addressed the weak spots from earlier attempts.
