Preparation tips

Practice every section, not just your weakest
Reading and Listening feed into Writing and Speaking prompts on the real exam — neglecting one section quietly limits the others.
Use short, frequent sessions
20–30 minutes of focused practice most days builds more consistent skills than one long session per week.
Check your Review Mistakes screen regularly
Patterns in what you get wrong are more useful than the raw score itself for deciding what to study next.
Sit a full mock test before test day
Practicing sections individually doesn't train stamina and section transitions the way a full-length mock test does.
