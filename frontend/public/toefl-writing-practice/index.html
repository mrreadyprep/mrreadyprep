Preparation tips

Answer the exact question asked
Academic Discussion and Email tasks are graded partly on whether you actually address every part of the prompt, not just on general fluency.
Plan for 30 seconds before you write
A one-sentence outline of your main point and 1–2 supporting reasons prevents responses that wander or run out of ideas halfway through.
Elaborate, don't just list
A short list of reasons scores lower than one or two reasons explained with a specific example or consequence.
Leave time to proofread
Most scoring loss on Writing tasks comes from avoidable grammar slips, not weak ideas — reserve the last minute to reread your response.
