Preparation tips

Take notes, don't transcribe
Write down key structure (topic, examples, contrast, conclusion) instead of trying to capture full sentences — you need to listen and think at the same time.
Listen for signal words
Words like "however," "for example," and "in contrast" mark the exact moments questions are usually built around.
Practice with real timing
Since replay isn't allowed on test day, train under the same one-pass conditions instead of pausing and re-listening.
Review wrong answers by format
Conversations, announcements, and academic talks test slightly different skills — track which format is actually costing you points.
