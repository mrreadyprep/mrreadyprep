Think Read in Daily Life is the "easy" part of the TOEFL Reading section? Not so fast. 📩⚠️

Yes, the texts are short and casual — emails, notices, social media posts. But you only get about 45-60 seconds per question, and the questions are full of traps.

Here's how to actually handle it:
1️⃣ Read the title and the first/last line first — that almost always tells you the purpose.
2️⃣ Scan for exact details (dates, prices, names) instead of re-reading the whole text slowly.
3️⃣ Watch for NOT / EXCEPT and swapped words like "required" vs. "optional" — one word can flip the correct answer.
