**Sahne 2 (2-6 sn) — Bağlam:**

**Sahne 3 (6-14 sn) — 3 maddelik strateji/bilgi (numaralı liste, yeşil):**
1️⃣ **Your total = the average of your 4 section scores**, rounded to the nearest half band (e.g. 5.125 avg → 5, but 5.25 avg → 5.5).
2️⃣ **Each half band maps to a CEFR level** (5-5.5 = C1, 4-4.5 = B2, etc.) — so a "5" tells you exactly what you can *do* in English, not just a number.
3️⃣ **Your old 0-120 score still shows too** — ETS keeps both on your report until January 2028, but the 1-6 scale is now the primary one.
