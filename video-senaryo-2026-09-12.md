## Sahne Kırılımı

**Sahne 1 (0-2 sn) — Hook:**
Görsel: telefon ekranında bir "interviewer" videosu soru soruyor, kişi kameraya konuşuyor, kafası karışık.
On-screen metin (kırmızı, ❌ ile): **"Talking for the full 45 seconds ≠ a good Interview answer ❌"**

**Sahne 2 (2-5 sn) — Bağlam:**
On-screen metin: **"Take an Interview: 4 questions, 45 seconds each, NO prep time, NO notes. One shot per question."**

**Sahne 3 (5-14 sn) — 3 adımlı strateji (numaralı liste, yeşil) — "IRT" formülü:**
1️⃣ **0-15 sec — Idea:** answer the question directly in your first sentence.
2️⃣ **15-30 sec — Reason:** give ONE reason or example — don't stack five weak ones.
3️⃣ **30-45 sec — Tie-in:** connect back to the question and close with a clear final sentence.

**Sahne 4 (14-17 sn) — CTA:**

Think talking non-stop for 45 seconds is enough to pass the TOEFL Interview task? It's not. 🎤⏱️

Take an Interview: you get 4 questions, 45 seconds each, with zero prep time and no note-taking. One shot per question — so structure matters more than speed.

Use the IRT pattern:
1️⃣ 0-15 sec — Idea: answer the question directly, right away.
2️⃣ 15-30 sec — Reason: give ONE solid reason or example.
3️⃣ 30-45 sec — Tie-in: connect back to the question and close clearly.

Rambling without this structure hurts your Organization score — even if your grammar is perfect.
